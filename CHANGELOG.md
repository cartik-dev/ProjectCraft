# Changelog

## 1.1.0 Alpha — 4 September 2026

First ProjectCraft 1.1 Alpha milestone.

### Added
- Fullscreen mode and improved responsive settings UI.
- Mobile touch controls, including quick access to the backpack.
- Animated water rendering with visible top and side faces.
- Larger continent/island generation and expanded sea areas.
- Underwater screen overlay with animated caustics and particles.
- Basic local water flow near the player, including downward filling and supported horizontal flow.
- Safer spawn-point selection with protection against spawning inside bedrock, water, or terrain.

### Fixed
- Water faces no longer rely on the legacy chunk water mesh.
- Water blocks can now be created by the local fluid-flow system.
- Chunk-boundary water mesh updates are handled when blocks change.

> Alpha status: gameplay systems are still experimental and may change before the stable 1.1 release.
